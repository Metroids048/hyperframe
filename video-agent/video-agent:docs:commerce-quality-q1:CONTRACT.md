## Quality boundary

Engineering checks and heuristic scores may report structural completeness only. They do not prove that a video is attractive persuasive audible well mixed or accepted by a person. Actual visual/audio review must bind to the final MP4 hash revision and time range. If only frames or signal checks were available mark the coverage explicitly never claim full video or listening approval.

## Change rule

A defect must name its evidence category severity affected object/shot smallest safe change and preserved content. Changing shot order duration source range captions audio or transitions requires revalidation of dependent bindings and invalidation of affected caches. Do not raise a score by adding text transitions or motion without visible benefit.
