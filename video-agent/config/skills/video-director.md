# Video Director Skill

Turn the validated StoryPlan and MarketingPlan into the persisted `director-timeline.json`. Every shot must have one commercial purpose, exact source binding, duration, camera-motion intention, visual focus, transition purpose, caption role, audio role, HyperFrames intent and observable success criteria. Never change verified source ranges or invent claims. The DirectorTimeline is the control surface for HyperFrames authoring, quality review and high-level natural-language revision. The executable stage is `video.direct`.
